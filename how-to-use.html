How to Use Convert Case

Convert Case provides simple tools for changing text capitalization, cleaning common formatting problems, working with hyperlinks, and checking basic text statistics. Follow the steps below to get the most from the tool.

Getting Started

  1. 1 Open the Home page.
  2. 2 Type your text into the editor or paste text from another application.
  3. 3 Choose the formatting or cleaning operation you need.
  4. 4 Review the result and use the Copy button when you are ready to use the converted text.

Text Case Conversion

Sentence Case

Use Sentence Case when you want the beginning of sentences capitalized while converting the remaining letters to lowercase.

Example: hello WORLD. THIS IS TEXT. becomes Hello world. This is text.

lowercase

Use lowercase to convert all alphabetic characters in the text to small letters.

Example: Hello WORLD becomes hello world.

UPPERCASE

Use UPPERCASE to convert all alphabetic characters to capital letters.

Example: Hello world becomes HELLO WORLD.

Capitalized Case

Use Capitalized Case to capitalize the first letter of each word.

Example: hello world from convert case becomes Hello World From Convert Case.

Title Case

Use Title Case when preparing headings or titles. Common short connecting words may remain lowercase according to the tool's title-case rules.

Example: the quick brown fox becomes The Quick Brown Fox.

Cleaning Text

Remove Commas

Use Remove Commas to remove comma characters from your text. This can be useful when preparing lists or text copied from another source.

Example: John, Mary, David becomes John Mary David.

Remove Extra Spaces

Use Remove Extra Spaces to reduce repeated spaces and tabs. The operation keeps the text's line structure while cleaning unnecessary spacing.

Example: Hello    world becomes Hello world.

Working With Hyperlinks

Text to Hyperlinks

Use this feature to turn supported email addresses and web addresses into clickable HTML hyperlink markup. This can save time when preparing text for emails, websites, documents, or other systems that accept HTML.

Example: ali@gmail.com can be converted into an HTML email link.

Hyperlinks to Text

Use this feature when you have HTML hyperlink markup and need to extract the readable link text or URL into a simpler text format.

This can be useful when copying content from HTML-based documents or cleaning text before reusing it elsewhere.

Text Statistics

The editor automatically updates basic statistics while you type or paste text.

Words

Shows the number of words detected in the text.

Characters

Shows the total number of characters in the editor.

Sentences

Provides an estimate of the number of sentences in the text.

Reading Time

Provides an estimated reading time based on the text's word count.

Editing Controls

Copy

Click Copy after completing your formatting or cleaning operation to copy the current text to your clipboard.

Clear

Click Clear when you want to remove the current text from the editor.

Undo and Redo

Use Undo to reverse a recent change and Redo to restore a change that was undone. Keyboard shortcuts are also supported for common undo and redo actions.

Sample Text

Use Sample Text when you want to quickly load example content into the editor and test the available formatting features.

Light and Dark Mode

Use the theme button on the main page to switch between light and dark appearance. Your selected theme can be saved in your browser for future visits.

Tips for Faster Text Formatting

  • Paste the complete block of text into the editor before choosing a formatting operation.
  • Use the case conversion buttons when you need to standardize capitalization across a larger block of text.
  • Use Remove Extra Spaces after copying text from documents or other sources that contain inconsistent spacing.
  • Use the hyperlink conversion feature when preparing email addresses or supported web addresses for HTML-based content.
  • Check the statistics displayed below the editor when you need a quick estimate of the text length.
  • Use Undo if a conversion produces a result you do not want.
