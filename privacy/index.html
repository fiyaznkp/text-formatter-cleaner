Privacy Policy

This page explains how information may be handled when you visit Fiyaz NKP or use its online tools.

1. Information we handle

Fiyaz NKP, available at fiyaznkp.com, provides online tools and related content. The information involved depends on how you use the website and its services.

  • Contact enquiries: The current contact form prepares an email in your email application. It does not directly submit the message to our website server.
  • Technical information: Hosting and other service providers may process information such as IP addresses, browser details, request times, and diagnostic logs.
  • Advertising information: Advertising services may use cookies or similar technologies, as described below.

Please avoid including confidential or sensitive information in contact messages or online tools unless necessary.

2. Online tools

Some tools may process text directly in your browser without sending it to our website server for their main function. This depends on how each tool works.

Your device, browser extensions, hosting infrastructure, and third-party scripts may operate independently. Do not assume that every input is confidential or that all processing happens locally.

3. Cookies, browser storage, and advertising

The website uses browser local storage to remember your light or dark theme preference.

Fiyaz NKP uses the Google AdSense advertising script. Google and its advertising partners may use cookies or similar technologies to deliver, measure, or personalize advertisements, subject to applicable settings, consent requirements, and law.

You can manage cookies through your browser settings. Where available, you can also review advertising preferences through Google Ads Settings. Blocking cookies or browser storage may affect some website features or third-party services.

For more information about Google's data practices, read Google's Privacy Policy.

4. Third-party services

The website uses GitHub Pages for hosting, Google Fonts for typography, and Google AdSense for advertising. These providers may process technical information according to their own policies.

External websites linked from Fiyaz NKP operate independently. Please review their privacy policies before sharing information with them.

5. Your choices

You can manage cookies and browser storage through your browser settings and adjust available advertising preferences. You can also contact us about privacy concerns or information you have sent directly.

Depending on your location, applicable law may provide additional rights concerning personal information. Requests involving information held by third-party providers may need to be directed to those providers.

6. Children's privacy

Fiyaz NKP provides general-purpose online tools and is not specifically directed at children. If you believe a child has sent personal information directly to us, please contact us so the concern can be reviewed.

7. Updates and contact

This policy may be updated when the website, its services, or applicable requirements change. The latest version will be published on this page with a revised date.

For privacy questions, visit our Contact page or email bestdeal4indians@gmail.com.
