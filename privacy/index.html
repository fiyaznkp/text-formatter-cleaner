Privacy Policy

1. Overview

Fiyaz NKP, available at fiyaznkp.com, provides online tools and related content. This policy explains how information may be handled when you visit the website, use its tools, or contact us.

2. Information we handle

The information involved depends on how you use the website and the services active on it.

  • Contact messages: The current contact form prepares an email in your email application. It does not directly submit the message to our website server.
  • Technical information: Hosting and other providers may process information such as IP addresses, browser details, request times, and diagnostic logs.
  • Advertising information: Advertising providers may use cookies or similar technologies, as explained in the advertising section.

Please avoid entering sensitive or confidential information into website forms or tools.

3. Online tools

Some tools may process your input directly in your browser, without needing to send it to our website server for the tool's main function. This depends on how each tool works.

Third-party scripts, browser extensions, and other services may operate independently. Do not assume that every input is confidential or that all processing happens locally.

4. Cookies and browser storage

The website uses browser local storage to remember your light or dark theme preference. This preference is stored in your browser.

Third-party services, including advertising providers, may use cookies or similar technologies for advertising, measurement, security, or other service functions.

You can manage cookies through your browser settings. Blocking cookies or local storage may affect some features.

5. Advertising

Fiyaz NKP uses the Google AdSense advertising script. Google and its partners may process information and use cookies or similar technologies to deliver, measure, or personalize advertisements, subject to applicable settings and consent requirements.

For more information, visit Google's Privacy Policy and Google Ads Settings.

6. Third-party services

The website uses services such as GitHub Pages for hosting, Google Fonts for typography, and Google AdSense for advertising. These providers may process technical information under their own policies.

External websites linked from this site have their own privacy practices. Please review their policies before sharing information with them.

7. Data retention and security

If you contact us by email, your message may remain in your email account and its backups until deleted. Third-party providers manage information they hold according to their own policies and retention practices.

Reasonable steps may be taken to maintain the website, but no online service or transmission method can be guaranteed to be completely secure.

8. Your choices and rights

You can manage browser cookies and site data, adjust available advertising settings, and contact us about privacy concerns or information you have sent directly.

Depending on your location, applicable law may give you additional rights concerning your personal information. Some information is managed by third-party providers, who may need to handle requests relating to their services.

9. Children's privacy

The website provides general-purpose online tools and is not specifically directed at children. If you believe a child has sent personal information directly to us, please contact us so the concern can be reviewed.

10. Changes to this policy

This policy may be updated when the website, its services, or applicable requirements change. The latest version will be published on this page with an updated revision date.

11. Contact

For privacy questions, visit our Contact page or email bestdeal4indians@gmail.com.
