1. Overview

Fiyaz NKP, available at fiyaznkp.com, provides practical online tools and related website content. This Privacy Policy describes how information may be collected, used, stored, or shared when you visit the website.

By using the website, you acknowledge this policy. If you do not agree with it, please discontinue use of the website.

2. Information we handle

The information involved depends on how you use the website and which third-party services are active.

  • Messages you prepare: If you use the contact form, the information you enter is used to prepare an email in your own email application. The current form does not itself submit that message to a website server.
  • Technical information: The hosting provider or other service providers may process technical information such as IP address, browser details, request timestamps, and diagnostic logs.
  • Advertising information: Advertising providers may use cookies, device identifiers, or similar technologies as described below.

We do not intentionally request sensitive personal information through the website's online tools. Please avoid entering confidential or sensitive information into forms or tools unless necessary.

3. How online tools handle your content

Some tools may process text or other input directly in your browser. For those tools, the input may not need to be sent to our own server for the tool's main function.

However, browser extensions, your device, hosting infrastructure, advertising scripts, and other third-party services may operate independently. Check the behavior of each tool before entering private or sensitive information.

Do not assume that every item entered on this website is confidential or that all processing is necessarily local.

4. Cookies and local storage

The website may use browser storage to remember preferences, such as your selected light or dark theme. This preference is stored in your browser using local storage.

Third-party services, including advertising services, may also use cookies or similar technologies to operate their services, measure activity, prevent fraud, or personalize advertising where permitted.

You can manage cookies through your browser settings. Blocking cookies or browser storage may affect some website features or third-party services.

5. Advertising and Google AdSense

This website may display advertisements provided by Google AdSense. Google and its advertising partners may use cookies or similar technologies to show, measure, or personalize ads, subject to applicable settings, consent requirements, and law.

Advertising practices can depend on your location, your consent choices, and your Google account or browser settings. We do not control all data processing performed by Google or other advertising partners.

Learn more about Google's practices at Google's Privacy Policy and manage relevant advertising settings through Google Ads Settings.

6. Third-party services and links

The website may rely on third-party services such as GitHub Pages for hosting, Google Fonts for typography, and Google AdSense for advertising. These services may process technical information according to their own policies.

The website may also link to external websites. We are not responsible for the privacy practices, content, or security of third-party websites. Review their policies before sharing information with them.

7. Data retention and security

Information handled by third-party providers is retained according to their policies and operational requirements. If you contact us directly by email, your message may remain in your email account and related backups until deleted.

We take reasonable steps within our control to maintain the website. However, no website, transmission method, or storage system can be guaranteed to be completely secure.

8. Your choices and privacy rights

Depending on your location and applicable law, you may have rights to request access to, correction of, or deletion of certain personal information.

  • Manage cookies and site data through your browser.
  • Adjust available advertising and privacy settings.
  • Contact us about a privacy concern or a request relating to information you have sent us directly.

Some technical records may be controlled by hosting or advertising providers rather than by us. Requests concerning those records may need to be directed to the relevant provider.

9. Children's privacy

This website is intended to provide general-purpose online tools and is not specifically directed at children. We do not knowingly seek to collect children's personal information through the website. If you believe a child has provided personal information to us directly, please contact us.

10. Changes to this policy

This Privacy Policy may be updated when the website's features, advertising arrangements, services, or legal requirements change. Updates will be published on this page, with the revision date changed when appropriate.
