ABOUT TEXT FORMATTER

About Text Formatter

Text Formatter brings practical tools for formatting, finding, cleaning and organizing text into one browser-based workspace. Use it to prepare everyday text for emails, documents, spreadsheets, websites, forms and messages.

Tools and features

Choose the section that matches the task you want to complete.

Text formatting

Change text to Sentence case, lowercase, UPPERCASE, Capitalized Case or Title Case. Remove commas and reduce repeated spaces when needed.

Find, Remove & Replace

Find a word or phrase, move between matching results, remove matches or replace them with different text.

Clean Text

Keep or remove numbers, words and special characters; tidy spaces; remove empty or duplicate lines; and extract numbers, email addresses or URLs.

Format Lists

Turn lines into bulleted or numbered lists, remove existing list markers, and add a prefix or suffix to items.

Text statistics

Check word, character, sentence and line counts, plus an estimated reading time while you work.

Editing and export

Undo or redo recent changes, try the sample text, copy the finished result or download it as a TXT file.

Some cleaning and replacement actions can remove meaningful content. Review the result before copying or downloading it.

Who can use Text Formatter?

It can help students, writers, office professionals, content creators, developers and anyone who regularly works with copied or unformatted text. The tools are intended for everyday text preparation, not specialist document editing.

How it works

Use the formatter in three straightforward steps.

1

Enter your text

Type in the editor or paste text from another application.

2

Choose a tool

Open the relevant section and select the formatting, cleaning, find-and-replace or list option you need.

3

Review your result

Check the updated text, make any further edits, then copy or download it.

Privacy and your text

Text formatting and editing operations are performed in your browser. The website uses Google Analytics to understand site usage and browser storage for certain preferences and tool convenience features. The formatter does not need an account to use. Please avoid entering passwords, payment details or other sensitive information into any online tool.
