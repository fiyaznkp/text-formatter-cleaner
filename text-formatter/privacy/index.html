Privacy Policy

Last updated: October 10, 2026

Introduction

This Privacy Policy explains how information is handled when you use Text Formatter & Cleaner, a browser-based text formatting and cleaning tool.

Please review this policy to understand the practices described below before using the website.

Information We Collect

You can use the main text formatting features without creating an account or submitting personal information through the tool.

We may receive limited technical or usage information through services used to operate and understand the website, such as analytics services. This information may include general information about visits, pages viewed, device or browser characteristics, and approximate usage patterns.

Text You Enter Into the Tool

Core operations—including changing capitalization, removing commas, removing extra spaces, and calculating basic text statistics—are designed to run directly in your browser on the text entered into the editor.

The website does not require you to submit text to an account or database in order to use these basic features.

You should nevertheless avoid entering confidential, sensitive, or highly private information into any online service unless you are comfortable doing so.

Google Analytics

The website uses Google Analytics to understand general usage. Depending on the service configuration, this may include pages viewed, broad usage patterns, and technical information associated with a visit.

Google may process information according to its own privacy policies and terms. You can learn more about Google's privacy practices through Google's official resources.

Cookies and Browser Storage

The website may use browser storage to remember certain preferences, such as the light or dark theme selected by the user.

Google Analytics may use cookies or similar technologies to collect information about website usage. The use of cookies depends on the service configuration and your browser settings.

Third-Party Services

Text Formatter & Cleaner uses third-party resources and services to provide website functionality and understand website usage. These include Google Analytics, Google Fonts, Font Awesome, and Tailwind CSS resources loaded through external content delivery services.

Third-party providers may process technical information when your browser requests resources from their services. Their own privacy policies may apply to that processing.

Children's Privacy

Text Formatter & Cleaner is a general-purpose website and is not specifically directed toward collecting personal information from children.

External Links

The website may contain links to external websites or services. Text Formatter & Cleaner is not responsible for the privacy practices or content of websites operated by third parties.

Security

We take reasonable steps to maintain the website and its functionality. However, no website or internet transmission can be guaranteed to be completely secure.

Changes to This Privacy Policy

This Privacy Policy may be updated when the website's features, services, analytics, or other practices change. Updates will be reflected on this page by changing the "Last updated" date.

Contact

If you have questions about this Privacy Policy or the website's privacy practices, please visit our Contact page.
