About Text Formatter & Cleaner

Text Formatter & Cleaner is a free online tool for formatting and cleaning text. Change capitalization, remove unwanted commas or extra spaces, and check text statistics from one simple page.

What is Text Formatter & Cleaner?

The formatter helps you prepare text for everyday tasks by converting letter case, cleaning up repeated spaces, removing commas, and showing useful text counts.

You can use it in a modern web browser without installing a separate application.

Features of the Text Formatter

Text Case Conversion

Change capitalization with Sentence Case, lowercase, UPPERCASE, Capitalized Case, or Title Case.

Text Cleaning

Remove commas and reduce repeated spaces in pasted text.

Text Statistics

View word, character and sentence counts, plus estimated reading time.

Works in Your Browser

Format text in your browser without installing software or creating an account.

Who is this tool for?

This tool is useful for anyone who needs to prepare, edit or check text quickly.

  • Students preparing assignments, notes and study materials
  • Writers and content creators formatting drafts and headings
  • Office workers and virtual assistants cleaning emails, lists and documents
  • Anyone who needs quick text cleanup or word and character counts

Privacy and your text

Text formatting and counting are designed to run directly in your browser, and you do not need an account to use the formatter.

The website may use analytics and browser storage for site functionality. Read our Privacy Policy .
