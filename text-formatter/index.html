How to use the Text Formatter

Format your text in three simple steps.

1

Enter your text

Type in the editor or paste text from a document, email or website.

2

Choose a format

Select the formatting option you need. The change is applied to all text in the editor.

3

Copy or download

Review your text, then select Copy or Download .TXT to save it.

What each option does

A

Sentence case

Changes the text to sentence-style writing.

Before: meeting NOTES. please review.

After: Meeting notes. Please review.

B

lowercase

Changes every letter to a small letter.

Before: HELLO WORLD

After: hello world

C

UPPERCASE

Changes every letter to a capital letter.

Before: hello world

After: HELLO WORLD

D

Capitalized Case

Makes the first letter of each word a capital.

Before: monthly project update

After: Monthly Project Update

E

Title Case

Capitalizes the first letter of each word for a title-style look.

Before: welcome to my website

After: Welcome To My Website

F

Remove commas

Deletes comma marks from your text.

Before: Sales, costs, and profit

After: Sales costs and profit

G

Remove extra spaces

Reduces repeated spaces and tabs and trims spaces at line ends.

Before: Hello    world

After: Hello world

How to find, remove or replace text

Make changes to words or phrases in three simple steps.

1

Enter a word or phrase

Type the text you want to find in the Word or phrase box.

2

Choose an action

Select Remove from my text, or enter replacement text and select Replace in my text.

3

Review the result

All matching occurrences are changed in the editor. Use Undo to reverse the change.

Examples

A

Find text

Search for: Calicut

Text: I live in Calicut. Calicut is in Kerala.

Result: Both matching occurrences are found.

B

Remove text

Search for: old

Before: old address

After: address

C

Replace text

Find: Calicut

Replace with: Kozhikode

Before: I live in Calicut.

After: I live in Kozhikode.

D

Previous and Next

When a search finds several matches, select Next to move forward or Previous to go back through them.

E

Refresh your search

After removing or replacing text, select Find matches again to search the updated text. Select Clear to reset the search and replacement fields.

How to clean your text

Choose a tool to keep the information you need or remove what you do not need.

1

Enter your text

Type or paste the text you want to clean into the main editor.

2

Choose a cleaning tool

Select a frequently used tool or open More cleaning tools for more options.

3

Check the result

The tool updates your text straight away. Review it before copying or downloading.

What each cleaning tool does

A

Keep only numbers

Keeps digits and separates groups with spaces.

Before: Price: $750,000

After: 750 000

B

Keep only words

Removes numbers and symbols, leaving words and spaces.

Before: Room 12 - Ready!

After: Room Ready

C

Keep letters & numbers

Removes punctuation and symbols but keeps letters, numbers and spaces.

Before: Room #12: Ready!

After: Room 12 Ready

D

Remove numbers

Deletes numbers from your text.

Before: Room 12 is ready

After: Room is ready

E

Remove special characters

Removes punctuation and symbols.

Before: Hello! How are you?

After: Hello How are you

F

Keep numbers & decimals

Extracts numbers, including decimal values, one per line.

Before: Price 25.50 and tax 3.25

After: 25.50
3.25

G

Remove decimal points

Removes the dot between digits.

Before: 250.75

After: 25075

H

Remove duplicate words

Removes a word repeated immediately after itself.

Before: This is is useful

After: This is useful

I

Remove duplicate lines

Keeps the first copy of each line.

Before: Apple / Banana / Apple

After: Apple / Banana

J

Remove empty lines

Deletes blank lines between text.

Before: Apple, blank line, Banana

After: Apple / Banana

K

Extract numbers

Finds numbers and lists them one per line.

Before: Order 25, quantity 3

After: 25
3

L

Extract email addresses

Finds email addresses and lists them separately.

Before: Contact sam@example.com today

After: sam@example.com

M

Extract URLs

Finds web links that start with http://, https:// or www.

Before: Visit www.example.com

After: www.example.com

N

Trim line spaces

Removes spaces at the beginning and end of each line.

Before:   Hello World  

After: Hello World

O

Replace tabs with spaces

Replaces each tab character with four ordinary spaces.

Before: Name [tab] Age

After: Name    Age

How to format a list

Turn plain lines into a bulleted or numbered list, or remove list markers.

1

Enter your items

Type or paste your list into the editor, with each item on a separate line.

2

Choose a list style

Select Add bullets to add bullet marks, or Add numbers to number each line.

3

Review your list

Select Remove bullets to remove existing list markers, then check the result.

Examples

A

Plain text

Apples
Bananas
Oranges

B

Add bullets

• Apples
• Bananas
• Oranges

C

Add numbers

1. Apples
2. Bananas
3. Oranges

D

Remove list markers

Removes bullet or number markers from the beginning of each line.

Before:
1. Apples
2. Bananas
After:
Apples
Bananas
