Privacy Policy

Last updated: August 30, 2026

Introduction

This Privacy Policy explains how Convert Case handles information when you visit and use the website. Convert Case is designed as a browser-based text formatting and cleaning tool.

By using the website, you acknowledge the practices described in this Privacy Policy.

Information We Collect

The main text formatting features do not require you to create an account or provide personal information.

We may receive limited technical or usage information through services used to operate and understand the website, such as analytics services. This information may include general information about visits, pages viewed, device or browser characteristics, and approximate usage patterns.

Text You Enter Into the Tool

The core text formatting operations are designed to run directly in your web browser. Features such as changing capitalization, removing commas, removing extra spaces, and calculating basic text statistics are performed on the text entered into the editor.

The website does not require you to submit text to an account or database in order to use these basic features.

You should nevertheless avoid entering confidential, sensitive, or highly private information into any online service unless you are comfortable doing so.

Google Analytics

Convert Case uses Google Analytics to understand general website usage and improve the website. Analytics may collect information about how visitors interact with the website, such as pages viewed, approximate usage patterns, and technical information associated with a visit.

Google may process information according to its own privacy policies and terms. You can learn more about Google's privacy practices through Google's official resources.

Cookies and Browser Storage

The website may use browser storage to remember certain preferences, such as the light or dark theme selected by the user.

Analytics or other third-party services may also use cookies or similar technologies as part of their operation.

Third-Party Services

Convert Case uses certain third-party services to provide website functionality and understand website usage. These may include Google Analytics, Google Fonts, Font Awesome, and Tailwind CSS resources loaded through external content delivery services.

Third-party providers may process technical information when your browser requests resources from their services. Their own privacy policies may apply to that processing.

Advertising

Convert Case may display advertising in the future. If advertising services are added, this Privacy Policy may be updated to explain the relevant data practices and technologies used by those services.

Children's Privacy

Convert Case is a general-purpose website and is not specifically directed toward collecting personal information from children.

External Links

The website may contain links to external websites or services. Convert Case is not responsible for the privacy practices or content of websites operated by third parties.

Security

We take reasonable steps to maintain the website and its functionality. However, no website or internet transmission can be guaranteed to be completely secure.

Changes to This Privacy Policy

This Privacy Policy may be updated when the website's features, services, analytics, advertising, or other practices change. Updates will be reflected on this page by changing the "Last updated" date.

Contact

If you have questions about this Privacy Policy or the website's privacy practices, please visit our Contact page.
