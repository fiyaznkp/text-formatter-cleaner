About Convert Case

Convert Case is a free online text formatting and cleaning tool designed to make common text-editing tasks faster and easier. It brings several useful text operations together in one browser-based editor.

You can use the tool to change capitalization, clean common formatting issues, and review basic text statistics. The goal is to provide a simple interface for people who regularly work with written text and need quick formatting tools.

Convert Case does not require an account for the basic text formatting features. Text transformations are performed directly in the browser.

What can you do with Convert Case?

Change Text Case

Convert text to Sentence Case, lowercase, UPPERCASE, Capitalized Case, or Title Case.

Clean Text

Remove commas and repeated spaces to make text cleaner and easier to reuse.

Check Text Statistics

View word count, character count, sentence count, and estimated reading time.

Copy Results

Copy your converted or cleaned text directly to your clipboard.

Undo and Redo

Undo previous changes or redo an action while working with your text.

Light and Dark Mode

Switch between light and dark appearance modes according to your preference.

Frequently Asked Questions

What is Sentence Case?

Sentence Case changes text so that the beginning of sentences starts with a capital letter while the remaining letters are converted to lowercase.

What is UPPERCASE?

UPPERCASE converts all letters in the selected text to capital letters.

What is lowercase?

lowercase converts all letters in the selected text to small letters.

What is Capitalized Case?

Capitalized Case converts the text to lowercase first and then capitalizes the beginning of each word.

What is Title Case?

Title Case applies title-style capitalization to words while keeping certain common short words lowercase when appropriate.

Can I remove extra spaces?

Yes. The Remove Extra Spaces option removes repeated spaces and tabs while maintaining the text's line structure.

Can I remove commas?

Yes. The Remove Commas option removes comma characters from the text currently in the editor.

Can I undo a conversion?

Yes. Use the Undo button or press Ctrl+Z on Windows and Linux, or Command+Z on macOS.

Can I copy the converted text?

Yes. After converting or cleaning your text, use the Copy button to copy the result to your clipboard.

Do I need an account?

No account is required to use the basic text formatting and cleaning features.
