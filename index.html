Frequently Asked Questions

What is Sentence Case?

Sentence Case changes text to lowercase and capitalizes the beginning of sentences.

What is Title Case?

Title Case applies title-style capitalization while keeping many common short words lowercase when appropriate.

Can I remove extra spaces?

Yes. Repeated spaces and tabs are reduced while preserving the text's line structure.

Can I convert URLs to hyperlinks?

Yes. Text to Hyperlinks detects common HTTP and HTTPS URLs and converts them into HTML anchor elements.

Can I convert hyperlinks back to text?

Yes. Hyperlinks to Text extracts URLs from HTML anchor elements and returns plain URLs.

Is my text uploaded to a server?

The core formatting and conversion functions run directly in your browser. They do not require an account or a form submission to a server.

Do I need an account?

No account is required for the basic features.
