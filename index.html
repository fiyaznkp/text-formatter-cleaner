About Convert Case

Convert Case is a free online text formatting and cleaning tool designed to help you quickly change the capitalization of text, clean common formatting issues, and check useful text statistics in one place.

What can you do with Convert Case?

Change Text Case

Convert text to Sentence Case, lowercase, UPPERCASE, Capitalized Case, or Title Case.

Clean Text

Remove commas and extra spaces to make text cleaner and easier to work with.

Check Text Statistics

View word count, character count, sentence count, and estimated reading time.

How to use Convert Case

  1. 1. Type or paste your text into the editor.
  2. 2. Choose the case conversion or cleaning option you need.
  3. 3. Review the converted or cleaned text.
  4. 4. Click Copy to copy the result.

Frequently Asked Questions

What is Sentence Case?

Sentence Case changes the text so that the beginning of sentences starts with a capital letter while the remaining letters are converted to lowercase.

What is UPPERCASE?

UPPERCASE converts all letters in the selected text to capital letters.

What is lowercase?

lowercase converts all letters in the selected text to small letters.

Can I remove extra spaces?

Yes. The Remove Extra Spaces option removes repeated spaces and tabs while maintaining the text's line structure.

Can I copy the converted text?

Yes. After converting or cleaning your text, use the Copy button to copy the result to your clipboard.
