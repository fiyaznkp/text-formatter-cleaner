Text Formatter & Cleaner

Convert Case is a free online text formatting and cleaning tool designed to help you quickly change text capitalization, clean common formatting issues, create clickable email hyperlinks, and check useful text statistics in one place.

What can you do with Convert Case?

Change Text Case

Convert text to Sentence Case, lowercase, UPPERCASE, Capitalized Case, or Title Case.

Clean Text

Remove commas and extra spaces to make text cleaner and easier to work with.

Email Hyperlinks

Turn email addresses into clickable links and convert hyperlinks back into ordinary text.

Check Text Statistics

View word count, character count, sentence count, and estimated reading time.

How to use Convert Case

  1. 1. Type or paste your text into the editor.
  2. 2. Choose a case conversion or cleaning option.
  3. 3. Use Email → Hyperlink to create clickable email addresses.
  4. 4. Use Hyperlink → Text to remove links and keep plain text.
  5. 5. Click Copy or Copy Rich Text depending on what you need.

Frequently Asked Questions

What is Sentence Case?

Sentence Case changes the text so that the beginning of sentences starts with a capital letter while the remaining letters are converted to lowercase.

Can I turn email addresses into clickable links?

Yes. Paste text containing email addresses and select Email → Hyperlink. The email addresses will appear as clickable links in the preview.

Can I convert hyperlinks back to text?

Yes. Paste or edit linked content in the hyperlink preview and select Hyperlink → Text to remove the links and keep ordinary text.

Can I remove extra spaces?

Yes. The Remove Extra Spaces option removes repeated spaces and tabs while maintaining the text's line structure.

Can I copy the converted text?

Yes. Use Copy for ordinary text or Copy Rich Text when you want to preserve clickable email hyperlinks.
